# Tiny World Roadtrip — Poki listing

## Final title

**Tiny World Roadtrip**

中文工作名：**小小世界环游记**

## Short description

Drive and sail across a toy-like 3D world, discover famous places, collect
postcards, and test your geography knowledge.

## Full description

Pack your bags for **Tiny World Roadtrip**! Drive a cheerful little travel car
across a colorful miniature world—and watch it turn into a boat when the road
meets the sea.

Follow your compass through 33 featured countries, stop at 54 famous landmarks
and natural wonders, spot 43 regional specialties, and fill a passport and
postcard album as you travel. Complete short three-stop roadtrips, take on
location-based geography quizzes, and unlock new paint colors for your car.
There is no race and no wrong route: explore at your own pace and make the whole
world your roadtrip.

## 项目简介

《Tiny World Roadtrip（小小世界环游记）》是一款轻松明快的 3D 世界旅行探索
游戏。玩家驾驶一辆遇水会自动变成小船的旅行车，在玩具模型般的世界地图上
自由穿行，造访 33 个重点国家、打卡 54 处世界名胜与自然奇观、发现 43 种
地域特色，并通过护照、明信片、三站式行程和地理知识挑战持续收集与成长。
游戏支持键盘、鼠标与触屏操作，并内置简体中文、英语、法语、巴西葡萄牙语、
意大利语和西班牙语。

## Controls

- Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to travel; `M` opens the world map; `Space`
  explores a nearby landmark.
- Touch: hold and drag anywhere on the playfield to travel; tap nearby
  landmarks and interface actions.

## Advertising placements

- Poki commercial break: before the first gameplay session, subject to Poki
  fill and frequency decisions.
- Poki and CrazyGames commercial break: after a completed knowledge challenge,
  at the natural pause before returning to travel.
- Rewarded ad: the player voluntarily selects the **Roadtrip Sunset** paint in
  Passport → Travel car paint. The paint is unlocked and equipped only when the
  rewarded-ad API returns success.
